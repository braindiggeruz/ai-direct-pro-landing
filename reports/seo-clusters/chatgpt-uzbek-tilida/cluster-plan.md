## Linking model

Every supporting page links to the transactional hub. The hub links back to the strongest supporting intents. Contextual links connect adjacent tasks: prompts ↔ students, access ↔ installation/payment, comparison ↔ business.

## Content and trust rules

- Independent-service disclosure is explicit where ChatGPT is mentioned.
- Product availability, price, currency and platform claims cite first-party documentation.
- No ranking, traffic, payment acceptance or business-result guarantees.
- No invented benchmark, customer case or unsupported integration claim.
- Security sections warn against unknown APK files, intermediaries and confidential prompt data.
