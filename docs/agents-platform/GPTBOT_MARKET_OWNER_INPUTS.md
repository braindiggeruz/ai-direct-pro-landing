# GPTBot Market — owner inputs

**Purpose:** the smallest decision/input set needed to move from audit to design, Pilot #1 and later public launch.
**Limit:** ten semantic blocks.
**Security:** do not put tokens, passwords, webhook secrets, chat IDs, phone numbers, private seller contacts, raw conversations or other PII in this file or Git. Store protected contact/credential material only in the existing owner-approved secret/contact system.

## Now

### 1. Product and naming decision

Owner provides one written approval or correction for:

- master brand: `GPTBot`;
- buyer product: `GPTBot Market`;
- seller program: `Sotuvchi by GPTBot`;
- canonical promise: “Напишите, что Вам нужно, — GPTBot найдёт подходящие товары в каталогах подключённых магазинов.”;
- current product category: Telegram product-finding assistant, not a public marketplace/payment platform;
- request-not-payment and invite-only seller truth.

**Needed output:** decision, approver role and date; no implementation claim. If the names change, include a legal/domain/trademark check owner.

### 2. Beachhead and non-goals

Owner chooses:

- one first category or narrow assortment pattern;
- intended city/fulfillment boundary if relevant;
- target seller profile and disqualifiers;
- whether Pilot #1 begins as one store’s smarter catalog or a cross-store experiment (recommended: one store first);
- explicit non-goals: payments, Click/Payme, escrow, logistics, public marketplace, broad-web search, regulated goods and CRM integrations unless separately approved later.

**Needed output:** one-page scope with one primary buyer job and seller job.

## Before design

### 3. Brand and creative guardrails

Owner approves or revises Direction B, “Warm Market Signals”:

- warm, modern local commerce;
- deep teal trust color, warm neutral surfaces and restrained coral action;
- real product photography;
- no robot heads, neural brains, magic wands, crypto neon, coins, fake reviews, fake stores or unsupported superlatives;
- restrained operational variant for seller/Owner tools.

**Needed output:** moodboard decision, forbidden references and the owner who approves final public assets.

### 4. Evidence access and reviewers

Owner designates roles—not credentials or personal identifiers in Git—for:

- authorized RU/UZ Telegram test-account capture on iOS and Android;
- authenticated read-only Owner Control Center capture;
- native Uzbek product-copy review;
- accessibility review;
- privacy/redaction review of screenshots;
- engineering reviewer for grounding/authority constraints.

**Needed output:** role assignment and safe evidence-handling path. Credentials remain outside Git.

## Before pilot

### 5. Consented Store Pilot #1 package

Owner supplies through a protected channel:

- one verified, consented business and its verified owner/operator;
- confirmation that the seller can maintain price/stock and answer within the proposed SLA;
- category fit and prohibited-goods check;
- consent for the exact pilot, data handling and any approved screenshots/case material;
- designated catalog operator if different from owner.

**Needed output in Git:** only `provided / approved / blocked` status and redacted business category; never private contacts or documents.

### 6. Catalog and commercial truth

Owner and seller approve:

- 10–30 real products for the first package;
- integer UZS price source and responsible updater;
- opening stock baseline and correction owner;
- names, categories, specs, variants and approved real photos/rights;
- fulfillment/delivery options, locations/coverage and what the seller actually supports;
- payment methods discussed between buyer and seller after the request, without implying GPTBot takes payment;
- return/complaint responsibility and product restrictions.

**Needed output:** an accepted import/QA package in the protected operational path plus a redacted acceptance record.

### 7. Service and incident ownership

Owner names through a protected contact system:

- seller response SLA and service hours;
- pilot support owner;
- incident lead and escalation path;
- daily review owner;
- catalog/stock correction owner;
- notification/DLQ review owner;
- pause/suspend and restart decision authority;
- privacy request/complaint owner;
- backup/rollback decision owner.

**Needed output in Git:** roles and coverage status only, no contact data. A tabletop rehearsal must pass before activation.

### 8. Pilot success, cohort and authorization

Owner decides:

- buyer cohort source and maximum scope;
- useful-result, zero-result, request-completion and seller-response expectations;
- acceptable stock-correction/incident thresholds;
- qualitative buyer and seller research plan;
- seller value/WTP question and continuation review;
- stop, iterate and expand rules;
- who can issue the separate explicit production activation authorization.

**Needed output:** signed metric/decision contract. Supplying inputs does not itself authorize onboarding, deployment or activation.

## Before public

### 9. Legal, trust and monetization decisions

After a successful private beta, owner supplies/approves:

- legal entity and merchant/platform responsibility language;
- public terms, privacy/retention, complaints, prohibited goods and support boundaries;
- seller verification/standards and removal policy;
- approved category/geography/store roster;
- payer, pricing package, billing responsibility and tax/accounting treatment;
- explicit decision whether payments are still out of scope (recommended until separately designed and approved);
- media/testimonial/case consent.

**Needed output:** legal/trust/commercial sign-off before public acquisition. No payment-provider credential belongs here.

### 10. Public capacity and go/no-go

Owner approves:

- retained responsive supply and minimum category coverage;
- support and incident capacity at planned traffic;
- performance/error/SLA/retention stop thresholds;
- acquisition caps and channel budget;
- public launch communications and incident status path;
- immutable last-known-good release/rollback owner;
- final security, accessibility, privacy, product, commercial and legal go/no-go participants.

**Needed output:** a signed Stage 10 checklist and separate public-launch authorization. Without it, remain in private beta.

## Current status

At audit completion, these blocks are requirements, not assumed answers. The safe next action is for the owner to complete **Blocks 1–4**. Blocks 5–8 gate Store Pilot #1. Blocks 9–10 are intentionally deferred until private-beta evidence exists.
