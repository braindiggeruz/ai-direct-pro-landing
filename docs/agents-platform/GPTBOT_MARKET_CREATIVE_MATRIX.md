4. Buyer/seller Telegram media and website proof.
5. Pilot launch, diary and result assets.
6. Launch/retention assets only after the corresponding roadmap gate.

Do not produce “public launch”, paid-ad or success-story assets in advance and then search for evidence to fit them.
