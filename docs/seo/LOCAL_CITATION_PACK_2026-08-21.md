On UTM: add `?utm_source=<directory>&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=citations-2026-08`
**only** where the directory renders a plain outbound link. Do not add UTM to a
citation whose value is the link itself — many directories strip or nofollow
parameterised URLs, and a tagged URL fragments the canonical signal. When in
doubt, submit the clean URL; GA4 will still attribute it as a referral.

## Rules for whoever does this

- One listing per directory. A duplicate listing splits the citation and can
  suppress both.
- Name, city and website must be byte-identical across every listing.
- Do not enter a phone number or a street address until the business actually
  publishes one. An inconsistent NAP is worse than an incomplete one.
- Do not buy links, do not submit to link farms, do not run mass outreach.
- Do not solicit a review in exchange for anything.
