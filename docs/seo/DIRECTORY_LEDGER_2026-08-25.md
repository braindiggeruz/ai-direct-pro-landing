   listing is visible to a logged-out visitor.

   read. It is never inferred from the platform.
3. A backlink is never counted in any report until the row says `LIVE`.
4. No automated or bulk submission. Every one of these forms is a business
   identity claim and belongs to the owner.
5. NAP must be byte-identical across every row once the phone exists. Enter it
   the same way every time or the citations cancel each other out.
