## Rules

1. A row moves to `LIVE` only when the profile URL has been opened and the
   listing is visible to a logged-out visitor.
2. `dofollow / nofollow` stays `unknown` until the live `rel` attribute has been
   read. It is never inferred from the platform.
3. A backlink is never counted in any report until the row says `LIVE`.
4. No automated or bulk submission. Every one of these forms is a business
   identity claim and belongs to the owner.
5. NAP must be byte-identical across every row once the phone exists. Enter it
   the same way every time or the citations cancel each other out.

## Why directories at all

Not for link equity. In the commercial SERPs measured for this market,
directories physically occupy **6 to 10 of the top 20 results** —
`goldenpages.uz`, `yellowpages.uz`, `glotr.uz`, `top.uz`, `prom.uz`, `tovar.uz`,
`flagma.uz`, `2gis.uz`, `yandex.uz/maps`. Being listed on them is being on the
result page. That is the whole argument, and it does not depend on any
assumption about how much a link is worth.
