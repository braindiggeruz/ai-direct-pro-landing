# White-hat authority package — Uzbekistan commercial pages

**Prepared:** 2026-08-01
**For:** `/uz/sayt-yaratish/`, `/ru/razrabotka-saytov-tashkent/`, and the AI product pages
**Status:** ready for human execution. Nothing here is automated, and nothing here should be.

---

## Why this exists

The GSC baseline is unambiguous: more than 150 URLs receive impressions and sit at
positions 60–95 for every commercial query the site targets. The pages are crawled,
indexed and served. They lose to older Tashkent domains — icorp, abc-design,
megagroup, upsoft — that have referring-domain profiles this site does not.

More on-page depth has already been tried. `/ru/razrabotka-telegram-bota-tashkent/`
received three rounds of enrichment and has 10 impressions and 0 clicks in 70 days.
On-page work is not the constraint. Links and real-world citations are.

Code cannot earn a link. This package is the executable half.

## Rules that bind every item below

- No paid links, no link exchanges, no PBNs, no mass-mailed outreach.
- No fabricated research, no invented client counts, no fake case studies, no invented awards.
- One personalised message per prospect. If it could be sent unedited to twenty
  recipients, it is spam and must not be sent.
- A directory that charges for a *listing* is fine if it is a real business directory
  people use. A directory that sells *link placement* is not.
- Every claim in an outreach message must be verifiable on the site the same day.

## 2. Linkable assets to build

Both must be real. If the data cannot be gathered honestly, the asset does not ship.

### Asset A — Uzbekistan AI and web-development demand benchmark

**Only from data that actually exists.** The measurements are already gathered in
`docs/seo/DEMAND_REALITY_CHECK_2026-08-01.md`: Google Ads volumes for location 2860
across the RU and UZ web-development and AI clusters, with competition and CPC.

Publishable findings, all of them measured:

- `sayt yaratish` (1,300/mo, LOW) alone outweighs the entire Russian Tashkent
  web-development cluster (~1,050/mo across nine keywords, several HIGH competition).
- The Uzbek AI product cluster measures ~14,150/mo at LOW competition.
- The bot-services cluster measures ~80/mo in both languages combined.
- Uzbek-language commercial search is systematically less contested than Russian.

That is a genuine, checkable story: *Uzbek-language search demand is larger and less
contested than the Russian-language demand most agencies target.* It has never been
published for this market.

Method section must state the source (Google Ads via DataForSEO, location 2860),
the date, and the known limitation — that keyword difficulty and intent are null for
Uzbekistan and that six-figure Russian figures are unreliable close-variant
aggregates. Publishing the limitation is what makes it citable.

Publish as a page with a downloadable table. Do not publish it as a "study" with a
sample size it does not have.

### Asset B — practical checklist: what a small business in Uzbekistan should ask before ordering a site

Derived from the process and rights sections already written on `/uz/sayt-yaratish/`,
expanded into a standalone checklist in Uzbek and Russian:

- Who the domain and hosting are registered to, and how to check
- What "turnkey" has to include before the price is comparable
- Which analytics must be connected on day one, and who owns the accounts
- What to test on a phone before accepting the work
- Which promises are red flags (guaranteed Google top, guaranteed sales)
- How to take the project to another team later

This is the kind of page local business media and chambers link to, and it costs the
business nothing to be honest about. It also reinforces the commercial page without
being a sales page.

## 4. Execution order

1. **Week 1 — P0 (6 items).** GBP, Yandex Business, 2GIS, LinkedIn, IT Park, amoCRM and
   Bitrix24 partner directories. Certain, permanent, no gatekeeper.
2. **Week 2–3 — build Asset A.** The data exists; it needs a page, a table and a method
   section.
3. **Week 3–4 — P1 editorial** using Asset A. Spot.uz and Kun.uz first.
4. **Week 4–6 — build Asset B**, then P1/P2 education and resource-page outreach.
5. **Ongoing — CS and EC.** Client credits and expert commentary compound slowly and
   cannot be rushed.

Track in a simple sheet: prospect, date contacted, channel, response, outcome, live URL.
Do not re-contact a non-responder more than once, and not sooner than three weeks.

## 5. What success looks like

Referring domains, not raw link counts. Ten real referring domains from directories,
two editorial placements and three client credits would materially change the
competitive position of `/ru/razrabotka-saytov-tashkent/`, which currently sits at
position 77 against domains that have hundreds.

Do not expect ranking movement inside 90 days. Recheck against the GSC baseline in
`docs/seo/RELEASE_2026-08-01_DEMAND_REALIGNMENT.md`.
