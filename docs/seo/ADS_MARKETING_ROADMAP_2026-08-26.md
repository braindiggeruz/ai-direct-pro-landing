# Roadmap — breaking into internet advertising and marketing services

Measured 2026-08-26, OpenSEO, location 2860 (Uzbekistan). **821 credits spent**
(2,309 → 1,488). Every number below is a live measurement, not an estimate.
Where something was not measured, it says so.

Sources: 2 keyword-metric pulls (109 terms), 1 discovery pull (311 unique terms
from 3 seeds), **13 live SERPs**, Search Console over 3 months, GA4, and the
free opportunity join.

---

## The headline

**The Russian-language "internet marketing services" market in Uzbekistan does
not exist at a size worth building for.** Every service term measures 10–40
searches a month:

| Term | Volume | CPC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `интернет маркетинг ташкент` | **10** | — |
| `услуги интернет маркетинга` | **10** | — |
| `агентство интернет маркетинга` | **10** | — |
| `маркетинг под ключ` | **10** | — |
| `digital агентство` | **10** | — |
| `маркетинговые услуги` | 20 | 5.06 |
| `лидогенерация` | 40 | 1.73 |
| `интернет маркетинг` | 20 | — |

Building service pages for these is building for ten searches a month.

**And the big `реклама` head is not digital at all.** The 6,600/mo `реклама`
family decomposes into outdoor and print:

`наружная реклама` 480 · `реклама баннер` 480 · `баннер реклама` 390 ·
`banner reklama` 320 · `reklama banner` 260 · plus билборд, LED, неоновая,
световая, на окнах, в лифтах, в маршрутках, в метро — and brand-recall junk
(`coca cola реклама`, `nike реклама`, `реклама тайд`). GPTBot sells none of it.

**There is exactly one large, winnable, digital advertising cluster in this
market, and GPTBot already owns two pages in it.**

**That matters more than anything else here: this is the one commercial cluster
GPTBot can enter without waiting for the owner's price facts.**

receives. `/uz/telegram-reklama/` is not in the top 20 for `telegram reklama`.

Both pages exist. Neither answers the cost question the SERP is built around.

## Phase 1 — Day 28 onward: the Telegram Ads cluster

**Gate:** the Day-28 read must not show a regression, and the `/ru/telegram-ads-uzbekistan/`
Watch read must be taken first.

**New URLs: 0.** Both pages already exist.

### 1.1 Rebuild `/ru/telegram-ads-uzbekistan/` around cost

The page sits at position 50 on `telegram ads узбекистан` and does not answer the
question its SERP is organised around. What every ranking competitor publishes,
and what this page should carry — all of it public, verifiable platform fact:

- the CPM floor and how the auction actually prices a campaign
- the three entry routes and their real minimums: direct (€2M deposit),
  authorised partner (from €250–1,500), and the TON / Stars cabinets
- which route applies to a business in Uzbekistan specifically, and why the
  €2M figure that dominates Russian articles is not the relevant number here
- what a channel needs to carry ads: 1,000+ subscribers, moderation rules,
  the 160-character limit
- what the budget does not cover: creative, landing page, the bot or form that
  catches the click
- **the honest limit** — when Telegram Ads is the wrong channel

Cite `lex.uz` article 6 on advertising language where relevant; the page already
does this correctly for the Russian targeting page.

### 1.2 Same treatment for `/uz/telegram-reklama/`

The Uzbek hub, now registered as the `telegram-uz` cluster by this release. Same
cost structure, written natively in Uzbek — not translated.

`telegram reklama` has competition **0.08** and three .uz domains in the top 8.
This is the most reachable commercial term measured in the entire space.

### 1.3 Register the cluster properly

Extend `telegram-uz` in `content/seo/intent-manifest.json` beyond the single
`telegram reklama` keyword once the Telegram cluster terms are measured in the
tracker, and add the Russian counterpart as a declared pair.

### Expected signal

Impressions on `telegram ads` / `ads telegram` variants within 7–14 days of
recrawl. Position movement on `telegram ads узбекистан` from 50 within 21–35 days.

## Phase 2 — conditional: `target nima`

**One article. Uzbek. Only if all three conditions hold:**

1. The Day-35 phrase rule passes — `sayt yaratish xizmati` improved, so exact
   coverage is a working mechanism on this site.
2. `smm nima` and `marketing nima` are proven to route sessions to money pages.
   The audit set this condition and it is **still unproven**: GA4 shows 0 key
   events from either, and the Day-42 internal-link read is the first evidence
   that will exist.
3. Nothing else is publishing into an open measurement window.

If they hold, this is the highest-CPC, lowest-competition term in the space
(5.02 at 0.01) with a SERP shape GPTBot has already beaten twice.

If condition 2 fails — the explainers bring readers who never reach a money page
— then `target nima` is a third article feeding an unproven mechanism, and the
correct action is to stop the explainer line entirely, not to extend it.

## Phase 3 — the constraint nobody can content their way past

The same four .uz domains appear across every commercial SERP measured:
**saytyaratish.uz**, **oqila.uz**, **kyoday.uz**, **wunder-digital.uz**. They win
on entity and placement, not on volume of content:

- `saytyaratish.uz` holds the knowledge-graph entity for `sayt yaratish` and
  shows Google reviews
- `oqila.uz` publishes prices on service pages and ranks with them
- directories occupy 6–10 of the top 20 in the measured commercial SERPs

Phase 3 is therefore not content. It is `docs/seo/DIRECTORY_LEDGER_2026-08-25.md`
rows 1–4 (no owner fact needed, can be done today), then rows 5–13 once a phone
exists, then the Organization / `sameAs` / citation work.

**If Phase 1 ships and positions do not move, the constraint is here, not in the
text — and no further article will change that.**

## What this roadmap deliberately does not propose

No new "интернет реклама" service page — 10/mo.
No "маркетинговые услуги" page — 20/mo, half the SERP is accounting.
No Google Ads service page — the SERP is Google's own properties.
No `лидогенерация` or `воронка продаж` article — Russian media wall, wrong audience.
No `konversiya nima` article — the word means something else in Uzbek.
No outdoor-advertising content — GPTBot does not sell it.
No new URL in Phase 1 at all.

The whole roadmap is **two existing pages rewritten and one conditional article.**
