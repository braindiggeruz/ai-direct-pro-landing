## 5. Implications for the stated goal

The chosen goal is commercial Tashkent service traffic. That goal remains valid, but the data relocates it:

1. **The commercial market is in Uzbek, not Russian.** `sayt yaratish` (1,300/mo, LOW competition) is the single best commercial target available and is currently unaddressed. Building a proper Uzbek web-development money page is the highest-value commercial action.
2. **The bot-services cluster should stop receiving investment.** It has no measurable search demand in either language. This is not a ranking failure — there is nothing to rank for. The ~140 pages built around it will not produce traffic regardless of quality.
3. **RU Tashkent web-dev is worth defending but not worth a campaign.** ~1,050/mo split across aged competitors (icorp, abc-design, megagroup) with the site at position 77–95. Winning it requires backlinks, and the prize is a few hundred visits/month.

Adjacent, not currently in scope: the AI product cluster is one to two orders of magnitude larger, has LOW competition, and the site already holds positions 6–8 there with pages that exist. It does not serve the commercial-services goal directly, but it is where the traffic is.
